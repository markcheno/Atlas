Atlas Explore spike

Drag to move, scroll to zoom. Each dot is a book, coloured by its area; numbers mark the areas, which are listed beside the map. Areas side by side get colours that are easy to tell apart; colours repeat, so the numbers say which area is which.

The areas

For each area, tick what holds. Choose an area to light up its books on the map.

The numbers

Counts, measures, timings and your ticks. No title, word or name from your library is in this box: it is what to share.
